## Refund Timelines

Amazon initiates a refund once your returned package is received. We process refund requests within 13 days of successful pickup.

### Processing Time by Payment Method

The time taken for your refund to be processed depends on your payment method:

Refer to the table below to know about time taken for refund to be processed, after Amazon has initiated the refund request.

**Prepaid orders**

| Payment Method for Refund | Time taken for Refund to be processed (For Amazon Fulfilled and Seller Fulfilled orders) |
| --- | --- |
| Amazon Pay Balance\* | 4 hours |
| Credit Card/ Debit Card | 5 Business Days |
| Net Banking Account (Credited to Bank Account) | 5 Business Days |
| UPI Linked Bank Account | 5 Business Days |
| Amazon Pay Later | up to 5 working days |
| Amazon Voucher | up to 4 working days |

**Pay on Delivery orders**

| Payment Method for Refund | Time taken for Refund to be processed (For Amazon Fulfilled and Seller Fulfilled orders) |
| --- | --- |
| NEFT to Bank Account | 5 Business Days |
| Amazon Pay Balance\* | 4 hours |
| Paper Cheque | Up to 10 business days |

- Please note that the refund is initiated by Amazon only once the item is picked up and reaches our Fulfilment Center. Please read our [Returns Policy](returns-policy.md) for more details.
- If you haven't received any refund update after 13 days of a successful pickup, please reach out to our Customer Service team for further assistance.
- If you haven't received your refund within the time frames mentioned above but your order status has been updated as refunded, please reach out to your bank with RRN (Refund Reference Number) displayed in the Refunds section in the Transactions history.

## Bank account for Refund

If you wish to receive the refund in your bank account, make sure to add/update the correct bank account details for refunds to be successfully processed. Follow the steps below to add a bank account for refund:

To add your bank account through website,

1. Go to Your Orders
2. Click on the order you want to return
3. Select Return or Replacement items option
4. Select 'Refund to your bank account'
5. Select 'Choose a bank account'
6. Select 'Add a new bank account', and enter your bank account details

## Amazon Pay Later Refunds

Your refund process depends on whether you've made any payments:

- If you have not paid any installment: We credit your Amazon Pay Later limit with the total order value.
- If you've already made a payment: We credit the amount back to your bank account and reinstate your credit limit.
- Visit Amazon Pay later help page to learn more

## Refunds for products purchased with EMI plans

If you've already paid an EMI, the amount paid as EMI will be refunded to your credit card and the loan will be cancelled.

**EMI closure Details:**

- Bajaj Finserv (BFL Card): Contact Bajaj Finserv or write to wecare@bajajfinserv.in
- Any other credit card: Contact your card issuing bank. Your bank may charge nominal pre-closure charges if you've already paid an EMI.

Visit Easy Monthly Installments (EMI) Help Page to learn more.

## Refund for Seller-Fulfilled Orders

- Any refund for orders sold and shipped by our seller partners needs to be initiated by the seller directly. Visit Seller-fulfilled orders help page to learn more about contacting sellers.
- The terms and conditions of refunds may vary from seller to seller and is available on Seller details page. You can access it by selecting Seller name on the product page.
- All seller fulfilled orders are covered by our A-to-Z Guarantee. Our Guarantee offers reimbursement and protection for instances when a seller is unable to resolve a missing or incorrect order. Visit [A-to-Z guarantee Help page](a-to-z-guarantee.md) to learn more.
