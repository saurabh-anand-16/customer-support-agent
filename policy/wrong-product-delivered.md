## 1. How to identify a wrong item?

To confirm if a wrong item was delivered to you, go to Your Orders. Check the product description and compare the details with the item you have received. If there is a mismatch in colour, size, fabric, design, model, or type, you have most probably received a wrong product.

> **Note:** Based on stock availability, certain items are delivered in different colours from the picture shown in the product description page. If this variation is mentioned in the product description page, this will not be considered as a wrong item.

## 2. What can I do if I receive a wrong item?

You can return or request a replacement of the wrong item. Please ensure the item is in its original condition to be eligible for a return. For more information, you can refer to the Help page: [Amazon Return Policy](returns-policy.md).

## 3. How do I initiate a return/ replacement for the wrong item?

Return/ exchange can only be requested within the return window of the product. Check the product details page to know if the order is eligible for return/ replacement.

- Go to Your Orders.
- Select the product that you want to replace.
- Click on Return/ Replace items.
- Choose a reason to replace.
- Continue and proceed as per the on-screen instructions.
- For more details on scheduling a return, refer to the page [How to schedule a Returns](how-to-schedule-a-return.md).

**Note:**

1. For non-returnable products, we might require a clear image for verifying that the product is damaged or defective. Visit the [Add Photos: Frequently Asked Questions](add-photos-faq.md) page to learn how to upload images.
2. Item will be picked up as per the scheduled pickup date and time, which will be communicated via email and visible in Your Orders.
3. If your address is not covered by our courier partners, you will need to return the item using any courier. Visit [Return Pick-up and Self-Ship Guidelines](return-pickup-and-self-ship.md) to learn more.

### Replacement/ Exchange

An exchange can be processed only if the stock is available with the same seller.

Once an exchange is requested, the new product will be shipped after original item is returned to us, or if it has been picked up by us for a return.

An exchange can only be requested once on the original order.

In case the exchange item's cost is more or less than the original item, we will neither charge nor refund the customer as the exchange order will be created with zero value similar to replacements.

## 4. What is the time frame for returning a wrong item?

The time window for initiating a replacement is the same as the return window for that product category. For example, if the product has a return window of 30 days, a replacement will also have to be initiated within the same period of 30 days. Visit [Replacement Policy](replacement-policy.md) to learn more.

## 5. What if I had kept the wrong item for a longer period before realizing?

Unfortunately, we will not be able to process a return / replacement request once the window is over.

## 6. Do I have to pay for return shipping?

Return Shipping charges are applicable only when customer self-ships the product. The amount will be refunded once the item (in its original state) is returned to us and the quality check is complete.

## 7. When will I receive a refund for the wrong item?

Allow up to 10 days for the returned item to reach our fulfillment center. Upon receiving your return, our team will conduct a thorough inspection. Once we are satisfied with our inspection, your refund will be processed within 5 business days. If you haven't received your refund after 13 days, you can contact Customer Service. The refund will be issued to the original payment method used for the order.

> **Note:** We recommend you complete the KYC process for a smooth completion of the refund process. You can do KYC verification via Digilocker or manual national ID upload and a selfie.
>
> Digilocker provides instant verification using One-Time Password. The manual document upload is verified manually and is processed within one business day.
>
> Accepted documents include a driving licence, passport, voter ID, and PAN Card.

## 8. What to do when I receive someone else's order?

If you receive a package or item that you didn't order, first confirm that it was not a gift that was sent to you. Check with friends and family to find out if they ordered the package. If you confirm that the package addressed to you wasn't ordered by you or anyone you know, report the package by contacting us.

To know more, check the Help page: [Received Someone Else's Order](received-someone-elses-order.md).
