## Details of Your Seller and Shipping Method

You can view seller details for any product directly on the product details page. This page will also help you understand whether the item is shipped by the seller or by Amazon.

If the product is shipped and delivered by Amazon, you'll find the following logo below the product details:

- Amazon Delivered

For more information, visit the following help pages:

- Amazon fulfilled orders
- Seller Fulfilled Orders

## Before You Return - Important Guidelines

- You must contact the seller within the applicable return window as mentioned in Amazon.in [Returns Policy](returns-policy.md).
- When you order from a marketplace seller who fulfills and delivers the product, your return is sent directly back to the seller. Return policies may vary between different sellers. You can view the seller's return policy on the seller profile page before purchasing an item.
- To return a seller-fulfilled order, raise a return request with the seller. Once the return is approved by the seller, arrange for the return pick-up with the seller.

## How to Return a Seller-Fulfilled Order

- Go to Your Orders
- Select the item you wish to return or replace.
- Click on Contact Seller. You will be directed to the Seller Messaging Assistant.
- Choose the appropriate option and initiate the conversation.

## What is A-to-Z Guarantee claim?

The Amazon A-to-Z Guarantee protects customers when they purchase items sold and shipped by a third-party seller who provides their own customer service directly to you.

A customer is eligible to file an A-to-Z Guarantee claim if:

- They have purchased physical goods which were sold and shipped by a third-party seller on the Amazon.in marketplace.
- AND in case of any issue, they have contacted the seller through their Amazon account and waited 48 hours for a reply.
- For more information on the eligibility criteria, visit [A-to-Z Guarantee help page](a-to-z-guarantee.md).

> **Note:** If the seller does not provide enough information to return your items or fails to address your query in 48 hours, you may file an A-to-Z Guarantee claim to seek help with your return.

For detailed guidance on how to file an A-to-Z Guarantee claim, visit the help page Request an A-to-Z Guarantee Refund
