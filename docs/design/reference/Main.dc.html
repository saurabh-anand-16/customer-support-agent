Hello, and welcome.

Let's sort out your return. This assistant handles returns, replacements, refunds and cancellations for Amazon.in orders, using the public policy pages and made-up orders, and it shows its work as it goes. Nothing changes until you say yes, and anything unusual goes to a person.

Choosing a customer is how you sign in here. A real store would ask for your login. This demo lets you pick a made-up customer instead, so you can try different situations. Everything the agent does is then done for that customer only.

Choose from the list, or type to search by customer id. Useful for trying your own situation.

What you can check as you go

  • Each answer names the policy page it came from.
  • Eligibility and money are worked out by code, not by the model.
  • You answer yes or no before anything is changed.
  • Exceptions go to a person. The agent never grants them.
