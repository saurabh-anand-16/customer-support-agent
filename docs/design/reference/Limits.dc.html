Limits and assumptions

This is a demonstration built on public policy pages and made-up data. It is not Amazon's system and it does not speak for Amazon.

Our decisions, not Amazon's

  • Which product categories map to which return policy.
  • Staff approval above ₹10,000, and for customers with more than 5 returns in 12 months.
  • When an item can still be cancelled.
  • Scope: returns, replacements, refunds and cancellations of orders fulfilled by Amazon. Seller-fulfilled orders and gift-card steps are left out.
  • Today's date is fixed at 17 Nov 2026 so results stay the same.

Known gaps

  • The router can misroute a message. On a first look it labelled 69 of 75 test messages as expected, against labels that are not yet reviewed.
  • The policy search thresholds are placeholders until the retrieval tuning is done.
  • For a bank-transfer refund the agent cannot check that a bank account is on file. It tells the customer to add one first.
  • Cheque refunds are held back: the policy says they are for exceptional cases.
  • The answer check is strict. A count the agent works out itself, such as "you have five orders", can be rejected and replaced with a plainer reply.
  • The manager page has no login. It is a demo.
  • If tracing to an outside service is switched on, message text can leave this app. It is off by default.
How to read this page: an item here is something we chose or something we know is not finished, not a hidden fault. The evaluation results will be added to the Developer page when the test suite is complete.
